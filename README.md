# Probashi-Footballers.com
**Tracking the global pipeline of Bangladeshi football talent.**

**Probashi Footballers** is a community-driven project that tracks players of Bangladeshi-origin playing worldwide. The word *Probashi* (প্রবাসী) is a Bengali word meaning *expatriate* or *non-resident*, used to refer to Bangladeshi people who reside abroad. Inspired by *Canucks Abroad*—a website tracking Canadian players abroad—this database bridges the gap between the global Bangladeshi diaspora and the national teams to identify the next generation of stars for the Men's and Women's Bangladesh national football teams. 

Launched in August 2025 with just a handful of listings, the database has grown to track hundreds of players thanks to an incredible community of fans. Together, we are bridging the gap between global talent and national pride—helping elevate Bangladeshi football to new heights.

---

## Data Sources & Methodology

To ensure accuracy and comprehensive coverage, our database is compiled from a variety of public and community-sourced channels, including:

- **Official club websites, rosters, and match reports**
- **National and regional football federation registrations**
- **Scouting reports, professional databases, and agent insights**
- **Verified social media accounts of players, clubs, and trusted scouts**
- **Direct community submissions from supporters like you**
