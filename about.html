About Probashi Footballers

Tracking the global pipeline of Bangladeshi football talent.

About This Website

Hi there! I’m Shifat, and welcome to Probashi-Footballers.com—a passion project built to map and champion the future of Bangladeshi football.

My journey started in Canada, where I watched the men's national team rise from an 8-1 heartbreak against Honduras all the way to the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar. That miracle run proved a transformative truth: dual-national recruitment changes the game.

Inspired by Canucks Abroad—a legendary fan-run site that tracked Canadian players worldwide—I looked toward my own homeland. With a massive global diaspora and trailblazers like Hamza Choudhury and Shamit Shome paving the way, it became clear that Bangladesh needed its own centralized database to discover and celebrate our global talent.

Launched in August 2025 with just a handful of listings, this platform has grown to track hundreds of players thanks to an incredible community of passionate fans. Together, we are bridging the gap between global experience and national pride—helping elevate Bangladeshi football to new heights.

Data Sources & Methodology

To ensure accuracy and comprehensive coverage, our database is compiled from the following trusted sources:

  • Official club websites, rosters, and match reports
  • National and regional football federation registrations
  • Scouting reports, professional databases, and agent insights
  • Verified social media accounts of players, clubs, and trusted scouts
  • Direct community submissions from supporters and followers like you
